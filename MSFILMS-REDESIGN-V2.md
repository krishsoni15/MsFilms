# MSFILMS — PREMIUM EDITORIAL PHOTOGRAPHY + FILM REDESIGN

## IMPORTANT
The visual design has been redesigned to move away from a standard "premium template" to a serious, high-end, art-directed photography and filmmaking studio portfolio.

## HIGH-LEVEL CREATIVE DIRECTION (House On The Clouds Reference)
- Visual storytelling
- Large-scale photography
- Photography-first hierarchy
- Editorial typography
- Featured stories
- Cinematic filmmaking
- Unconventional image compositions
- Non-linear visual rhythm
- Strong project presentation
- Large visual moments
- Curated asymmetric grids

## THE RHYTHM
We shifted the layout flow from a structured text-block-based startup landing page to an immersive photographic magazine layout:
`IMAGE → STORY → IMAGE → FILM → STORY → IMAGE → PERSONALITY → CONTACT`

## COLOR SYSTEM
- Background: Warm ivory `#F4F1EB`
- Dark text/elements: Near black `#171717`
- Secondary text/accents: Warm stone `#AAA39A`
- White: `#F8F7F3`

## TYPOGRAPHY
- Serif: `Instrument Serif` (for hero, major section headings, emotional statements)
- Sans-serif: `Inter` (for navigation, body, labels, forms, UI metadata)

## MAIN INTERACTIVE ADDITIONS
1. **Custom Desktop Cursor:** A minimal circle that expands to reveal contextual text (`VIEW STORY`, `PLAY FILM`, `LET'S TALK`) when hovering over imagery or buttons.
2. **Autoplay Cinematic Video Poster Fallback:** Architecture designed for `/media/hero.mp4` with a beautiful static image poster.
3. **Films Player & Lightbox Modal:** Fullscreen overlay modal playing films when triggered.
4. **Mouse-Follow Hover Images:** Under Services, hovering a row reveals a floating photographic preview following the cursor on desktop.
5. **Photo Journal Grid:** An asymmetrical vertical strip layout mixing portrait and landscape images to break linear symmetry.
6. **Form Animations:** Stripped-down elegant underline inputs with floating label animations and smooth success screen.
7. **Global Film Grain:** Subtly overlayed noise to give a physical, cinematic texture to the viewport.
