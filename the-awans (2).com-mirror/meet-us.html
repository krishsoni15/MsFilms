let's chat!

Our story began with a shared passion for photography and videography that ignited in Hashim and Ameer back in 2012. They couldn't resist the allure of capturing life's moments and creating memories, which led them to embark on a fun-filled journey of making hilarious YouTube videos together.

In 2019, fate intervened when they received the opportunity to shoot their first wedding for a dear family friend. That magical day marked the turning point. Hashim and Ameer instantly fell in love with the art of capturing love stories and making them come alive through their lenses.

Since that pivotal moment, we've been on an exhilarating ride of continuous growth and self-improvement. We've dedicated ourselves to honing our skills, refining our brand, and nurturing our exceptional team. It's been an incredible journey, one enriched by the beautiful couples we've had the privilege to work with and the remarkable individuals who've joined our team along the way. We feel truly fortunate to have transformed our passion into a thriving reality, creating lasting memories for couples like you.
